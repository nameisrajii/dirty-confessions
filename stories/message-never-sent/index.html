The message I never sent

I wrote it a hundred times. I sent it zero.

There is a draft in my messages that is three years old. No timestamp visible anymore, just his name at the top of it like an address to nowhere.

It starts with "Hey." Because after everything, what else do you say?

We were never officially anything. That's what made it so easy to leave and so impossible to get over.

I never sent it because a small part of me decided that a beautiful unsent thing is safer than a sent thing that gets left on read.
