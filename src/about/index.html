About Dry Humps

Dry Humps is a home for anonymous confessions — hidden feelings, complicated love, and the things people never say out loud.

Every story is written in the first person and shared without a name attached. Some are true, some are adapted. All of them are meant to make you feel a little less alone in the things you never told anyone.

New confessions go up every week.

Got one of your own? Send it in.
